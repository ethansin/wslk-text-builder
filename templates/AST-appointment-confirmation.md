Hook-ups:
-14 electrodes with white paste are placed on your head. They are no harm to you, just to identify the sleep stages while you sleep.
-3 EKG attached to your heart/chest area.
-2 Leg EMG attached to your left and right legs.
-2 Elastic bands are placed around your chest and stomach to check your breathing.
-1 Oximeter attached to a finger, this is to check the oxygen level in blood while patient sleeps.
-1 Snore microphone attached to neck area.
-1 Sleep Position indicator.
-1 Nasal cannula attached to the nose. (PSG diagnostic study only)
-1 Transducer airflow sensor attached to the nose. (PSG diagnostic study only)
-1 Mask covered the nose and some air going into the nose, this is for opening up the airway while you sleep (CPAP Titration study only)


The hook up process takes about 45-60 minutes; after the hook up, we will do some calibrations with you, then you may go to sleep. We will wake you up around 05:30 – 06:00 am the next morning, and it takes about 15-20 minutes to take off everything, so you will be ready to leave the office around 6:30 am.
